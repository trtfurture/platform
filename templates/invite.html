01Join the programmeRegister your name and M-PESA number. We create a unique invite link for you.

Programme rules

  • The reward is KES 1,000 for one genuinely new institution, not for each click, account or form submission. A school already submitted to the platform, an existing advertiser/customer account, or your own institution is not eligible as a new referral.
  • The institution must open your invite link and submit its first-time listing within 30 days, then pay for its first eligible VIP or VVIP promotion. Standard directory listings do not trigger a reward. The same newly referred administrator may submit other distinct institutions through the same partner, but existing advertiser accounts and duplicate institution entries do not qualify.
  • Payment must be confirmed by Kenya Schools using a valid transaction reference. A screenshot or promise to pay is not confirmation.
  • If more than one partner link is opened before the institution submits its listing, the most recently opened valid link is attributed. There is a seven-day clearance period after payment confirmation. Refunds, reversals, duplicate institutions, self-referrals, false details and abuse are excluded.
  • Rewards are paid manually by M-PESA to the registered phone number after clearance and administrator verification.
  • Rewards are funded by Kenya Schools from its advertising revenue. The referred institution is not charged an extra referral fee.
  • Kenya Schools may pause or investigate suspicious referral activity. Any change to reward terms will apply prospectively and be communicated on this page.

Referral rewards may have tax implications. Any applicable tax treatment or withholding will be handled as required by Kenyan law.
