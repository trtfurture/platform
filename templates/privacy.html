YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

How Kenya Schools handles information shared through this website.

Information you provide

When you contact us, enquire about an institution, request a school portal, submit an institution profile or advertise accommodation, we collect the details entered in the relevant form. This may include your name, phone number, email address, institution details, photographs and message.

How information is used

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, review and manage listings, contact advertisers, administer requested promotions, maintain customer records and operate the website.

Institution and accommodation listings

Information approved for publication may appear on public profile and directory pages. Contact details supplied for a listing may be displayed so that parents, learners and other visitors can contact the institution or advertiser.

Cookies and visitor statistics

The website uses essential session cookies for secure administration and core site operation. Optional site analytics are off until you choose to allow them through the cookie banner or Cookie settings. When enabled, analytics use a random browser identifier to estimate visits and understand which public pages are useful; visitors' IP addresses are not stored in the visitor statistics table. Clearing browser cookies or using another device may result in a visitor being counted again. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer.

Referral programme

If you open an invitation link, we use a signed referral cookie for up to 30 days to remember the referral code when a new institution submits a listing. If the institution later purchases an eligible paid promotion, the referral code is used to calculate the partner reward. Referral partners provide their name and phone number so we can administer the programme and send any approved reward. A referral code is stored in a signed browser cookie for up to 30 days after an invitation link is opened. We may share the minimum necessary payout details with the mobile-money provider to send a reward, and keep transaction records for accounting, fraud prevention and applicable legal requirements. The referred institution is not charged an extra referral fee. You can contact us about your personal information using the contact details below.

Sharing and access

Enquiry details may be shared with the institution selected by the visitor so it can respond. Information is accessible to authorised platform administrators for website operations, customer support and listing management. We do not publish private administrator credentials.

Security and retention

We use access controls and technical safeguards intended to protect information. Information is retained for as long as it is needed for the purposes described above, to manage active listings and records, or to meet applicable legal requirements.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information submitted through the website, request correction of inaccurate details or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Photographs and student information

Only upload photographs you are authorised to share. Do not submit identifiable photographs or personal information about children unless you have the required permission and lawful basis.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact form{% if publicSettings.get('contact_email') %} or email {{ publicSettings.get('contact_email') }}{% endif %}.

This notice is general information about the website's current features. It does not replace legal advice or any additional notices required by applicable data protection law.
