YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy & responsible use.

This initial policy summary explains the basic information practices of the SCHOOLS platform. It must be reviewed and completed before public launch.

Information submitted to us

Institution representatives may submit institution details, contact information and campus photographs. Property advertisers may submit accommodation and contact details. These submissions are reviewed before publication.

Images of children

Do not upload identifiable student images unless you have the necessary permission and lawful basis. Institutions are responsible for ensuring that photographs and videos they supply are properly authorised.

Enquiries

Information submitted through an enquiry form may be shared with the institution the visitor selected so that it can respond. The production enquiry workflow and retention schedule must be completed before launch.

Cookies and analytics

The platform uses essential session and security cookies for administration. Any optional analytics, advertising trackers or marketing communications must be disclosed and configured with appropriate consent.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the platform operator through the official contact details published on the website once launch contact channels are configured.

This is a launch-draft policy, not legal advice. Finalise it against the Kenya Data Protection Act and applicable ODPC guidance before collecting real user information.
