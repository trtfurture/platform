ABOUT KENYA SCHOOLS

Better discovery.
More informed choices.

Kenya Schools is an education directory for finding and exploring schools, colleges and universities across the country.

OUR PURPOSE

Make education options easier to explore.

We bring institution profiles, locations, learning levels, admissions contacts and campus information into one accessible place. We also help schools and higher-learning institutions strengthen their digital presence.

Paid placements are labelled. Profile recognition and institutional confirmation are separate from promotional packages. We encourage every visitor to confirm fees, programmes, admissions and availability directly with an institution.

Kenya Schools is a product of Toror Technology and Innovations Limited.
