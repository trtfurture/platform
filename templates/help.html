HOW TO USE THE DIRECTORY

Start broad. Then narrow down.

You do not need to fill every search option. Enter a school name, course, education level or town. Leave county set to “Nationwide · all counties” to search across Kenya.

Explore institutions
What can I type into the search box?

Try an institution name, a town such as Nakuru, an education level such as secondary, or a programme such as nursing. You can combine a keyword with a county filter.

Do I have to choose a county?

No. County is optional. Keep “Nationwide · all counties” selected to search nationwide, or choose one county to narrow the results.

How should I use an institution profile?

Check its location, institution type, ownership, education levels, accommodation and contact information. Use the official website or phone details to confirm current admissions, fees, vacancies and requirements.

What do the verification labels mean?

“Institution confirmed” means the platform has marked the profile as confirmed. “Recognised listing” is a separate directory status. A sponsored or featured label indicates paid visibility and does not mean the institution has been independently endorsed.

When will my review appear?

Reviews are placed in a private moderation queue. They become visible only after an administrator approves them. Rejected reviews remain hidden from the public profile.

How much does it cost to advertise an institution?

A standard directory listing is free. VIP costs KES 3,000 for 30 days, KES 5,500 for 60 days or KES 7,500 for 90 days. VVIP costs KES 6,000 for 30 days, KES 11,000 for 60 days or KES 15,000 for 90 days. Paid visibility starts after payment is confirmed and the institution profile is approved and published. See all advertising packages.

How do I add or correct an institution?

Use “List your institution” to submit a profile. For corrections to an existing profile or other help, send a message through the contact page.

How does Invite & Earn work?

Join the referral programme to receive a unique link. You earn KES 1,000 when a genuinely new institution uses your link, submits its first listing within 30 days and completes its first eligible paid VIP or VVIP advert. Payment is confirmed by our team, followed by a seven-day clearance period and M-PESA payout. There is no extra referral fee charged to the institution. See the full programme rules.

Can I change cookie choices later?

Yes. Use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page to reopen your preferences. Essential cookies remain active; optional analytics can be switched off.
