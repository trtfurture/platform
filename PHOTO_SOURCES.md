# SCHOOLS photograph register

The application uses authentic photographs of the named institution only. It does not generate photographs and does not substitute an unrelated school or campus. Every mapped item is linked to its source and author in the site's image caption. The image is loaded from Wikimedia Commons; this project ZIP contains the source URLs and attribution data, not copies of the image files.

## Original five seed profiles: what has and has not been mapped

- Alliance High School: authentic Commons photos are mapped.
- Strathmore School (Lavington): **no open-licensed exact-match photo was confirmed for this build**. The app shows a name-based placeholder rather than use a Strathmore University photo or a copyrighted photo copied from another website. The school's official site has a photo gallery; a written reuse permission or an image supplied by the school can be added later.
- University of Nairobi: authentic Commons photos are mapped and one is also used as the homepage search background.
- Kenyatta University: authentic Commons photos are mapped.
- Kenya Utalii College: **no open-licensed exact-match photo was confirmed for this build**. Its official media gallery contains real college photography, but no reuse licence was found. The app therefore does not copy it without permission.

## Safety and data behaviour

- Photo mappings apply only to the exact corresponding slug already present in the database. They do not create new institutions, change an institution's category or ownership, or publish/hide listings.
- Existing user-uploaded cover photos and galleries are preserved.
- The retired generic `school-hero.webp` asset is removed from listing covers/galleries. When no authentic photo is available, the site uses the institution's initials, not an AI image or unrelated stock photo.
- The homepage search background uses the real University of Nairobi entrance photo, with attribution visible on the hero.
- This file is a source register, not a guarantee that a third-party image host will always be online. Wikimedia-hosted files load from Wikimedia's servers, so users' browsers need internet access to display them.
